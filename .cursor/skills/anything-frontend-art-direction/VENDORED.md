These upstream paths were left out so the PULSE repository does not carry large binaries or fonts. The omission is not a relicense.

| Path | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `examples/` (`shiomachi-film`, `dead-air-fm`) | Example sites, about 6 MB. Their embedded fonts are SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Shippori Mincho B1, Cormorant Garamond, Noto Sans JP, Archivo Black, Space Mono) and are not part of the MIT grant. No font files were copied. |
| `docs/shiomachi-film.png`, `docs/dead-air-fm.webp` | Example screenshots, about 1 MB. |
| `data/typography/catalog.sqlite3` | About 440 KB. The CSV notes (`findings.csv`, `usage-depth.csv`) and `data/typography/README.md` are included. |
